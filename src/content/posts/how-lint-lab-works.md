Lint Lab is how students work with us. It is not a course and it is not a fee-based
internship. It is a seat at the bench next to people building real security products.

## Who it's for

Students who are curious enough to open something up and find out how it works.
Branch and year matter less than the spark. If you've ever stayed up taking apart a
binary, a router or a broken deployment, you'll fit in.

## What you do

You pick a track (security, reverse engineering, systems or AI) and work on one of our
live projects with a mentor. Tasks are matched to your level and you get honest
feedback on them.

## What you get

- **Real experience** on industry-style projects, the kind you can talk about in an
  interview.
- **An experience certificate** from Lint, an Udyam-registered company, which anyone
  can check on our [verification page](/verify/).
- **A place in our network.** When paid client work comes in, active lab members are
  the first people we call.

## Staying active

People move on, and that's fine. We keep a short list of active members and stay in
regular contact with them. If you go quiet for a term, you move to alumni, and you can
come back whenever you're ready.

## How to join

Send us a short note about what you want to work on and something you've built or
broken. The [Lint Lab page](/lab/) has the details.
