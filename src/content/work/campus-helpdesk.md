## The problem

Students ask the same questions every term: fee dates, syllabus changes, who signs
which form. The answers exist, but they are spread across notices, PDFs and group chats.

## The approach

Collect department information into one indexed source and answer questions over it
with a retrieval pipeline, so every answer points back to the document it came from.

## Status

Early research. We are exploring it as a product for colleges.
